Calabar Rapids

At Calabar Rapids, our mission is to give every adventurer an unforgettable experience on the water. Our expert guides prioritize safety while you enjoy stunning landscapes and challenging rapids. Come ride with us!

History

Calabar Rapids was founded in 2015 by a group of adventure lovers who wanted to share the beauty of Nigeria's rivers with the world. Starting with just a handful of rafts and a passion for exploration, the company has grown into a leading provider of white water rafting experiences.
